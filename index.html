DESIGNED FOR RESEARCH WORKFLOWS

From a single video to traceable experiments

RodentStudio is built around how researchers document sessions, inspect tracking and evaluate results—not just around a single detection output. Availability varies by assay.

▦

Organize studies

Tasks, experimental groups, animal IDs, session numbers and notes.

⌁

Track movement

On-device Core ML for supported pose workflows and classic centroid tracking where applicable.

◎

Calibrate & review

Set arenas and regions of interest; inspect keypoints, trajectories and questionable frames.

⇩

Export data

Structured tracking, review and analysis outputs for downstream research workflows.

◷

Work session by session

Preserve local records and repeat visits to an experiment as needed.

◈

Scientific transparency

Clearly scoped workflows and outputs that researchers can independently validate.

Research note： RodentStudio is a research aid, not a diagnostic tool. Scientific claims require manual validation, suitable experimental design and appropriate statistics.
