Privacy Policy

How RodentStudio handles videos, session information and generated results.

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Applies to RodentStudio for macOS, release candidate 0.6.3.

In brief: The source code reviewed for this release does not include user accounts, in-app ads, developer-operated analytics, or an app-operated upload service. Imported videos and generated results are processed locally. Verify these statements against the final distributed binary before publishing.

1. Controller and contact

RodentStudio is developed by Ziqing Shi. For general questions and privacy inquiries, visit the Support page. The public issue tracker is not a secure channel for personal or sensitive data; ask for private follow-up without posting such information.

2. Data handled by the app

You may choose research videos and enter project names, experimental conditions, animal identifiers, groups, notes, calibration points, review annotations, and other session metadata. The app processes these locally to generate pose estimates, tracking data, figures, and export files, depending on the workflow.

3. Collection and transmission

Based on the reviewed version, RodentStudio does not transmit this research content to a developer-operated server, require an account, integrate advertising SDKs, or include developer-controlled analytics. This statement describes app behavior and does not govern other services or actions you independently choose.

4. Local storage and permissions

Workspace information and run artifacts are saved in the app's macOS Application Support storage. Source files and exports may also reside in locations you select. macOS file access is subject to the permissions you grant when selecting files or folders. The app uses local Core ML inference for supported pose tracking workflows; RodentFlow also includes a classic centroid-tracking workflow.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you or the operating system remove it. You can manage exported files with Finder. Removing an item within the app may not delete the original source video or all generated files. Back up your research files, and verify the associated file locations before deleting projects or uninstalling the app. The developer cannot remotely erase locally stored data.

6. Third parties and website hosting

If you share a report or video using another service, that service's own privacy terms apply. This documentation website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process website connection and usage information according to the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website does not implement a custom analytics script or an information-submission form.

7. Security and changes

Keep macOS updated, safeguard any confidential research material, and follow applicable institutional data policies. This policy may be updated if future releases add new data processing or transmission features. The date and text on this page will be updated accordingly.

Privacy statement prepared from reviewed application source files and the bundled privacy manifest. The publisher must confirm the final App Store build and all integrated third-party components before submission.
